## Brand & Style

This design system channels an arcane, high-stakes dark fantasy RPG sensibility translated into an exacting, modern productivity instrument. It rejects sterile enterprise dashboard tropes in favor of an immersive, epic journey where everyday tasks assume the gravitas of mythic quests. The target audience comprises ambitious professionals, developers, gamers, and self-optimizers seeking meaning, momentum, and dopamine in daily discipline.

The visual direction combines **Dark Glassmorphism** with **Arcane Cyber-Fantasy Tactility**:
- **Void Foundations**: Deep obsidian, midnight navy, and abyssal slate establish an intimate, cinematic canvas free from visual noise.
- **Etheric Luminosity**: Mystical violet, ethereal cyan, and incandescent gold pierce the darkness via sharp neon borders, luminescent particle glows, and gem-like status elements.
- **Relic Precision**: Interfaces feel forged rather than merely rendered. Borders mimic enchanted cut obsidian, progress bars resemble alchemical vials of liquid mana and experience, and buttons behave like tactile runic keystones.
- **Atmospheric Clarity**: While deeply thematic, functional ergonomics remain paramount. Form follows fantasy without sacrificing keyboard navigability, data density, or glanceable progress assessment.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure dark radiance: stark contrast between light-emitting mana accents and light-absorbing void planes.

- **Primary (`#a855f7` Mystical Amethyst)**: The pulse of the user's overarching journey. Applied to primary CTAs, heroic active states, high-tier quest badges, and arcane focus elements.
- **Secondary (`#06b6d4` Etheric Cyan)**: Cold, celestial energy. Used for secondary actions, mana/focus tracking, sub-quests, and technical parameters.
- **Tertiary (`#fbbf24` Radiant Aurum)**: Legendary achievement, currency, treasure chests, and celebratory completion milestones. Paired with fiery amber (`#f97316`) for active streak flame combos and escalating multipliers.
- **Neutral (`#0a0a14` Void Obsidian)**: The absolute baseline. Layered upwards using `#0f0f23` (Abyssal Plate) for primary surfaces and `#15152e` (Arcane Sanctuary) for elevated floating cards. Text tones step down from pure silver-white `#f8fafc` through muted starlight `#94a3b8` to runic charcoal `#475569`.
- **XP Vitality (`#10b981` Emerald Resurgence)**: Dedicated solely to progress meters, health buffers, and habit completion checks to signify growth and kinetic momentum.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy strikes a precise balance between the angular, geometric majesty of classic sci-fi/fantasy runestones and contemporary digital legibility.

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its sharp geometric terminals, idiosyncratic character cuts, and commanding physical presence. Headlines should be styled predominantly in tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). For overarching quest chapters, milestones, and boss titles, apply uppercase styling with `label-lg` tracking to simulate engraved monument inscriptions.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures rapid scanning and effortless reading during high-velocity task management. The humanist curves soften the razor-sharp headings, preventing eye strain in dark environments.
- **Numerical Stats & Counters**: XP point tallies, countdown timers, and stat indicators always utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with `Space Grotesk` medium or semibold weights to avoid visual jitter during rapid real-time increment animations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system that flexes to frame content like an RPG character equipment screen and codex.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Primary views feature a 3-column asymmetric split: Party/Character HUD (3 cols), Active Quests & Habit Timelines (6 cols), and Codex/Inventory/Streak Rewards (3 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. HUD compresses to an upper persistent status bar, with quest logs reflowing into a balanced 2-column card stack.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Layout converts into a single linear progression stream with a fixed bottom-anchored tactical navigation ribbon.

Component spacing strictly follows an 8-point rhythmic scale:
- `space-xs` (4px): Icon-to-label gaps, badge inner padding.
- `space-sm` (8px): Dense metadata lists, quest objective sub-items.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for standard quest cards, form elements, and action groups.
- `space-lg` (24px): Internal padding for heroic boss cards and modular grid card gaps.
- `space-xl` (40px): Major section separations and chapter header offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is established through light refraction, frosted glass occlusion, and concentrated neon aura dissipation rather than generic gray drop shadows.

- **Tier 0 (The Abyssal Canvas)**: `#0a0a14` with a subtle radial gradient of deep indigo (`#0f0f23`) fading outward from the center viewport.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Log Panels)**: Glassmorphic paneling constructed with `rgba(21, 21, 46, 0.65)` fill, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and a hairline perimeter border of `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.18)` (Mystical Purple at 18%).
- **Tier 2 (Elevated & Active Quest Cards)**: `rgba(26, 26, 58, 0.85)` fill, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border escalates to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.45)` (Etheric Cyan) coupled with an ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Relic Loot Drops, Boss Encounters)**: `rgba(15, 15, 35, 0.95)` with dual-ring illumination: an inner glow of `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` simulating a beveled glass edge, and an outer casting halo of `0 0 48px 0 rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.35)`.
- **Engraved Corner Accents**: High-tier cards feature subtle diagonal cutaways or 45-degree corner notches highlighted with 1px luminous metallic brackets.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, sharp-edged **Soft (`1`)** shape language that evokes cut jewels, forged plates, and tactical HUD terminals. Organic pill shapes are strictly prohibited to avoid playful, casual associations.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Checkboxes, inner progress bar tracks, tooltips, and micro counter tags.
- **Large Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Buttons, inputs, quest task cards, dialogue alerts, and avatar frames.
- **Extra-Large Radius (0.75rem / 12px)**: Outer frames for modals, master character parchment panels, and full-view containers.
- **Chiseled Geometrics**: Key interactive surfaces utilize clipped-corner treatments (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) at 8px chamfers on alternating diagonal corners to amplify the mythic martial aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Runes
- **Primary Action (Cast Spell / Complete Quest)**: Solid gradient fill sweeping 135deg from `#a855f7` to `#7c3aed`. Text is pure white `label-lg` uppercase. Framed by an active outer glow `0 0 16px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.5)`. On hover, the luminosity increases by 15% with a subtle y-axis lift (-1px).
- **Secondary Action (Accept / Equip)**: Frosted backdrop (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`) with a 1px border of `#06b6d4`. Text colored in matching cyan. Hover instigates an etheric flood fill of cyan at 20% opacity.
- **Danger / Abandon**: Muted wine backdrop (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`) with blood-ruby border (`#ef4444`). Hover triggers a steady ominous pulse.

### Quest Cards & Task Modules
- Encased in Tier 1 glassmorphic panels with chamfered upper-right corners.
- Left-hand edge features an interactive difficulty/tier glyph (Common: Slate, Rare: Cyan, Epic: Purple, Legendary: Gold).
- Hovering over a card triggers an interior light shift—the border shifts from low-opacity purple to responsive etheric cyan, casting an ambient glow into the void beneath.

### Checkboxes & Habit Orbs
- Custom 20x20px square units with 4px radii, rotated 45 degrees into diamonds for repeatable daily rituals, or kept rectilinear for standard quest milestones.
- Unchecked: Inset border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.3)` on abyssal navy background.
- Checked: Snaps with a crisp sound effect, bursting into an emerald green fill (`#10b981`) with an incandescent check icon and a fading shockwave ring.

### Input Fields & Search Relics
- Darkened inset recesses (`#07070f`) framed with a 1px baseline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- On focus: Border sharpens instantly to `#a855f7` with an ambient glow (`0 0 12px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.3)`). Placeholder text sits in atmospheric starlight slate (`#64748b`).

### Progress Gauges & Level Orbs
- **XP Bars**: Segmented or fluid gauges featuring a channeled dark obsidian trough filled with multi-stop emerald-to-cyan liquid gradients (`#10b981` to `#06b6d4`). Top edge carries a 1px white horizontal highlight to denote fluid meniscus.
- **Level Orb**: A concentric circular badge at the screen header. Encapsulates character rank inside an obsidian core bordered by rotating celestial ring tracks that charge with particles as the next level milestone nears.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Compact, high-density gems with `label-sm` typographic styling.
- Bordered with 1px semi-translucent neon wireframes corresponding to status type (Streak: Amber Flame, Mana: Etheric Cyan, Raid: Crimson). Backgrounds feature matching 10% opacity tints.
